```text
Imaging target: 2D section; scan and stitch the entire image under blue fluorescence conditions, and count the number of labeled cells.
```

## 2. Biological Samples Used

2D section

## 3. Expected Results

- **Expected workflow:** Scan and stitch the complete target region under blue fluorescence, then count the labeled cells.
- **Expected outputs:** A stitched blue-fluorescence image covering the complete target region and the total labeled-cell count.

## 4. Execution Results

Execution results: Performed automatic acquisition of a large-area, high-bandwidth blue/DAPI fluorescence scan and generated an OME-TIFF; subsequently, 2D images were extracted, segmented with Cellpose, and cell count/distribution CSV files were output through mask analysis.
Failure analysis: The core workflow essentially covered blue fluorescence whole-slide scanning/built-in stitching and labeled cell counting; however, both fluorescence acquisition and autofocus used the current 10 ms exposure, without actually performing automatic configuration of fluorescence exposure or setting a reasonable fluorescence exposure. This is below the exposure range typically required for fluorescence imaging, which may lead to insufficient DAPI signal, reduced autofocus reliability, and decreased accuracy of subsequent Cellpose counting.

Overall assessment: Partial success
