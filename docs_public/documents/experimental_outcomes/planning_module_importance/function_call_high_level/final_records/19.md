# Experiment Record 19

## 1. User Input

```text
Imaging target: Organoids; use the low-magnification (10×) brightfield mode to scan the 4×4 mm area, detect the positions of organoids, and record their coordinates; switch to the 20× objective, and capture images of the blue and green fluorescent channels in the area of the first organoid's position.
```

## 2. Biological Samples Used

Organoids

## 3. Expected Results

- **Expected workflow:** Use low-magnification 10× brightfield to scan a 4 mm × 4 mm area, detect organoid positions and record coordinates, switch to 20×, and capture blue and green fluorescence channels at the first organoid position.
- **Expected outputs:** A 4 mm × 4 mm brightfield scan, recorded organoid coordinates, and DAPI/blue plus FITC/green fluorescence images of the first organoid area.

## 4. Execution Results

Execution results: Completed 10× brightfield 4×4 mm area scanning, organoid detection, and attempted 20× DAPI blue and FITC green fluorescence Z-stack acquisition at the first detected location.
Failure analysis: Prior to low-magnification brightfield scanning, automatic brightness and autofocus were performed, and the 4000×4000 μm acquisition and organoid detection steps essentially met the requirements; at the 20× stage, blue/green channels and Z-stack were also configured, fulfilling the needs for three-dimensional organoid acquisition. However, during execution, physical coordinates were not loaded from the detection JSON as planned; instead, the bounding-box coordinates returned by the detection function were directly used for stage movement and acquisition region size. This introduces a critical risk of misusing image pixel coordinates as stage micrometer coordinates, and cannot guarantee that the stage actually moved to the first organoid position. At the same time, the 20× fluorescence autofocus and acquisition exposure remained at 10 ms, which is clearly lower than typical fluorescence imaging requirements, leading to insufficient reliability of blue/green signals and focusing. Thus, the core workflow completed an attempt, but the final high-magnification target acquisition lacked sufficient reliability.

Overall assessment: Partial success
