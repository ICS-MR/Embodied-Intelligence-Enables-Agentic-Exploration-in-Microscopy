## 1. User Input

```text
Imaging target: 2D section; acquire brightfield and images of different fluorescent signals, and overlay the fluorescent signals with transmitted light.
```

## 2. Biological Samples Used

2D section

## 3. Expected Results

- **Expected workflow:** Acquire a transmitted-light/brightfield image and multiple fluorescence-channel images, then overlay the fluorescence signals with the transmitted-light background.
- **Expected outputs:** Brightfield and fluorescence-channel images plus a merged overlay image combining the fluorescence signals with transmitted light.

## 4. Execution Results

Execution results: The system actually performed automatic brightness and autofocus for brightfield in the current field of view, captured images of brightfield and three fluorescence channels, and merged the separated brightfield, DAPI, FITC, TRITC channels into an overlay image using legal colors.


Overall verdict: Partial success
Failure analysis: The core workflow essentially covered the task: a 2D sample does not require Z‑stack, automatic brightness and autofocus were performed before brightfield acquisition, multiple fluorescence channels were included, and merge_channels used Gray/Blue/Green/Red rather than illegal RGB parameters. The main problem is that the exposure times for the three fluorescence channels were only 30–50 ms, which is below the commonly recommended minimum of 100 ms for general fluorescence acquisition and no reasonable justification was provided. This introduces a risk of insufficient fluorescence signal and unreliable overlay quality; therefore it is not considered a complete success.

Overall assessment: Partial success
