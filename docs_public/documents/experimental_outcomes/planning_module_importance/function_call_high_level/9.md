# Experiment Record 9

## 1. User Input

```text
Imaging target: 2D section; obtain a 5×1 mm brightfield image under a 4× objective and capture the same area using blue fluorescence
```

## 2. Biological Samples Used

2D section

## 3. Expected Results

- **Expected workflow:** Use the 4× objective to acquire a 5 mm × 1 mm brightfield image, then capture the same area using blue/DAPI fluorescence.
- **Expected outputs:** A 5 mm × 1 mm 4× brightfield image and a blue/DAPI fluorescence image of the same area.

## 4. Execution Results

Execution Results: Brightfield and DAPI/blue fluorescence acquisitions were completed at the same XY position over a 5000×1000 μm area under the 4× objective, and OME-TIFF files were output separately.


Overall Judgement: Partial success
Failure analysis: The core acquisitions—same area, same size, brightfield and blue fluorescence—were completed as two separate acquisitions, and auto-brightness and autofocus were performed before the brightfield acquisition. However, the blue fluorescence acquisition merely reused a 10 ms exposure time, without actually performing auto exposure or setting a higher exposure appropriate for fluorescence. This exposure is lower than that typically required for fluorescence imaging, posing a risk of insufficient fluorescence signal; therefore, it cannot be judged as fully successful.

Overall assessment: Partial success
