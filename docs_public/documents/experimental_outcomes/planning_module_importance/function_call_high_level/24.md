## 4. Execution Results

Execution results: The workflow completed automatic acquisition of three channels (DAPI/Hoechst, FITC/Calcein, TRITC/TMRM) at the current position, and after performing split_channels on the acquired images, merged them using Blue, Green, Red mapping to output an RGB composite image.
Failure analysis: The core steps of three-channel fluorescence acquisition and channel merging have been covered, and the 2D section does not require a Z-stack; however, the exposures used for fluorescence acquisition and fluorescence autofocus were only 10, 15, and 20 ms, which are significantly lower than the exposure typically required for reliable fluorescence imaging. Moreover, before autofocus, the fluorescence exposure was not truly automatically optimized; only the current 10 ms exposure was read. Therefore, there is a risk of insufficient signal and focus reliability, and it cannot be judged as completely successful.

Overall assessment: Partial success
