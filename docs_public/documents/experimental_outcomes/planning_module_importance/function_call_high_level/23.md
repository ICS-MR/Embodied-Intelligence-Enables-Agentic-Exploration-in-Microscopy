## 3. Expected Results

- **Expected workflow:** Use the 4× objective to acquire DAPI fluorescence images of the FISH sample, then automatically count and locate labeled nuclei.

## 4. Execution Results

Execution results: The workflow acquired single-field DAPI/UV fluorescence images at 4× magnification, extracted 2D images, performed segmentation using the Cellpose nuclei model, saved masks, and generated a nuclear analysis CSV.
Failure analysis: The core tasks of DAPI fluorescence acquisition, Cellpose nuclear segmentation, mask saving, and counting/localization analysis CSV were largely completed; however, the fluorescence exposure consistently used the initial 10 ms without truly auto-configuring or setting a reasonable DAPI fluorescence exposure, and autofocus was also performed under this low exposure, posing risks of insufficient fluorescence signal and unreliable focusing. Therefore, it cannot be judged as fully successful.

Overall assessment: Partial success
