### frap
- FRAP operation control: Start and stop the active FRAP workflow state required for bleaching and cell analysis while keeping the session available.
- Targeted bleaching: Perform bleaching at specified field-centered micron coordinates, including repeated targeting across multiple planned positions.
- Cell discovery: Detect usable cells in the current field of view and return their identifiers with center-relative coordinates for targeting or analysis.
- Cell contour mapping: Extract usable cell boundary approximations as fitted ellipse point sequences for spatial planning around detected cells.
- Preferred usage: Use for FRAP workflows that start and stop FRAP, detect cells, extract contours, and photobleach selected field-centered micron coordinates. Spatial patterns can be executed as sampled coordinate trajectories derived from fitted contours or generated paths. Bleaching intensity is controlled by sampling point spacing.
